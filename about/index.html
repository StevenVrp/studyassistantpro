Study Assistant Pro

StevenVRP developed Study Assistant Pro for independent learning. Taki Jiang leads the Table of Elements project.

The study desk includes task planning, reminders, a focus timer, flashcards, and a mistake journal. The periodic table pairs electron configurations with orbital shapes. Equationarium pairs an AP Calculus BC workbench with 34 interactive physics experiments for IPhO preparation.
